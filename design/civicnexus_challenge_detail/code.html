Urban Water Runoff in Old Gwalior

location_on Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh
calendar_today Submitted Oct 12, 2024

Problem Context

During the monsoon season, the historic district of Old Gwalior experiences severe waterlogging due to an antiquated drainage system that fails to handle rapid surface runoff. This results in significant disruption to local commerce, accelerated degradation of heritage structures, and emerging public health concerns due to stagnant water accumulation.

The primary issue stems from the impermeable concrete surfaces laid over the past decade, combined with a lack of localized retention basins. The current infrastructure channels all runoff directly into the primary arterial drains, which rapidly exceed capacity.
