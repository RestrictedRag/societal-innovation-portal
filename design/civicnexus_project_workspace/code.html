Sustainable Urban Runoff Mitigation

Developing bio-filtration systems utilizing native flora to reduce heavy metal contamination in municipal stormwater networks.

Project Summary

This initiative seeks to address the critical issue of heavy metal runoff into the River District's municipal water supply. By engineering modular bio-filtration units integrated with native wetland flora, the project aims to capture and neutralize pollutants before they enter the main waterways.

Currently in Phase 2 (Prototyping), the team has successfully cultivated hyperaccumulator plants in a controlled environment and is preparing for a small-scale deployment in the north industrial zone.
